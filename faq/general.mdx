Find answers to the most common questions about Cora. If you don't see your question here, email [support@cora.ai](mailto:support@cora.ai).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is Cora a support chatbot?">
    Not exactly. Cora is an AI-powered concierge that works behind the scenes to streamline communication, coordinate tasks, and escalate issues to the right Cora team contact when needed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Cora replace my account team?">
    No. Cora complements your existing account team by handling routine workflows and enabling faster, more proactive support. When a high-touch interaction is needed, your team is still just a message away.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I know if I have access to Cora and the workspace?">
    You'll receive an email or Slack message from Cora that includes a link to your workspace. If you're unsure, reach out to your account team.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I'm not receiving emails from Cora. What should I do?">
    Make sure to check your spam, junk, or promotions folders for messages from Cora. If you still don't see them, reach out to [support@cora.ai](mailto:support@cora.ai) for assistance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is my data secure with Cora?">
    Yes. Cora aligns with our enterprise-grade security standards. It only accesses the data needed to support your experience and is built with safeguards that ensure confidentiality, security, and compliance.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I click a link and can't log in?">
    Make sure you're using the same email address that received the Cora message and the same login method you used previously. If you're still having trouble, contact [support@cora.ai](mailto:support@cora.ai).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

If your question isn't answered here, reach out to [support@cora.ai](mailto:support@cora.ai) and we'll be happy to help.
