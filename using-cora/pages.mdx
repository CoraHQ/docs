---

Ask Cora in chat for an HTML page, such as a renewal page. Cora saves it on **Artifacts**.

It opens inside Cora. It is not a public website. Share it the same way as a document, including with someone at the customer account.

Images you upload in chat are saved with the page. A link to another site opens in a new tab. A link to a section of the page stays on the page.

**Export PDF** downloads the page as it looks when it opens. Anything that appears only after a click is not in the file. Asking Cora to email the page sends that PDF.

A page can include its own layout and behavior. It does not load scripts, frames, or forms from other sites.

Mark it as a template the same way as a document or presentation.
