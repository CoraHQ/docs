Turn Home and every account page into a dashboard built for how your team works. Ask Cora for the page you want, then pin it.

## Build one

Ask in chat. For example: _"Build our homepage: accounts that need attention, tasks waiting on review, and agent activity."_

Cora saves it as a **Dashboard** artifact. See [Data apps](/using-cora/data-apps) for what that dashboard can show and do.

You can also start from the **Home** template. On **Analytics**, click **Create Dashboard**, then **Add** on **Home**. That saves the dashboard.

Ask Cora to change it if you want. Open the dashboard and click **Chat**. For example: _"Add a renewal queue."_ You can ask before you pin it, or after.

Open it from **Artifacts** and pin it to **Homepage**.

## Pin it

On **Artifacts**, open the dashboard and click **Pin**. Choose **Homepage** or **Account pages**. The same menu still pins a dashboard to **Analytics**.

A workspace has one Home dashboard and one account dashboard. If one is already pinned, the menu shows **Replaces the current dashboard**. Choosing it asks **Replace the homepage dashboard** or **Replace the account page dashboard**. Click **Replace** to switch.

A private dashboard asks **Share with the organization**, then **Share and pin**, because everyone will see it. If another dashboard already holds that pin, Cora asks you to replace it before this one is saved.

The menu shows a check next to **Homepage** or **Account pages** while that dashboard is pinned. Choose it again to remove the pin and bring back the standard Home or **Overview**.

## Everyone sees their own book

The same Home dashboard opens for the whole team. What it lists depends on who is looking.

- An account owner sees **My book**, the accounts they own.
- A manager from [account ownership sync](/getting-started/managing-accounts#account-ownership-sync) sees **My team's accounts**.
- Anyone else sees the whole workspace, with no label.
- Someone who is both an owner and a manager sees **My team's accounts**.
- Someone with a role and no accounts sees zeros, not the whole workspace.

Account team changes show up after the next sync.

<Note>
  This is a starting view, not a permission. It does not limit what anyone can open.
</Note>

## Account pages

One dashboard renders for every account, scoped to the account you are on. It replaces **Overview**.

Open that dashboard from **Artifacts** when no account is selected and it shows **No account selected**, with **Choose an account**, so you can preview one account before you pin it. On an account page, that choice is not there.

## Who can do what

Admins pin to Home and account pages. Viewers see the pinned pages. Actions on those pages follow the same rules as [Data apps](/using-cora/data-apps#who-can-act). Customers in the portal keep the pages they already have.
