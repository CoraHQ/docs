Artifacts are the saved outputs Cora keeps for you: briefs, dashboards, presentations, pages, surveys, checklists, files, and links. Agents and chat create most of them. You open them later from the **Artifacts** page.

The brief on an account's **Overview** is set up from Accounts. See [Account briefs](/using-cora/account-briefs).

For how artifacts sit next to signals and agents, see [Core Concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts).

## What an artifact is

An artifact is a saved output attached to an account or to the workspace.

Open **Artifacts** from the nav to see everything in the workspace. Open an account and go to its **Artifacts** tab to see only that account's items.

Artifacts are not agent runs, and they are not signals. A run is one execution of a workflow. A signal is an event Cora detected. An artifact is the thing you keep.

## Types

Use **Filters** on the **Artifacts** page to show specific types. The filter labels are:

- **Document.** Markdown you can read in Cora, such as a meeting prep doc, a QBR summary, or an account brief. Chat and agents both write these. You can mark one as a [template](#templates).
- **Dashboard.** A data app Cora builds over your data: live charts, tables, and actions you can take in place. Ask Cora in chat when you want one. You can pin a dashboard to **Analytics**. See [Data apps](#data-apps).
- **Presentation.** An in-app slide deck, such as a QBR or a book review. Ask Cora in chat when you want one. Chat shows a preview. Reopen it from **Artifacts** with the **Presentation** filter, then **Present** from the player. You cannot pin a presentation to **Analytics**; that stays dashboards. You can mark one as a [template](#templates).
- **Page.** An HTML page you ask Cora to build, such as a renewal page. Ask Cora in chat when you want one. You can mark one as a [template](#templates).
- **Survey** and **Survey Response.** A **Survey** is the reusable template. A **Survey Response** is a completed set of answers, attached to the account you sent the link for. Building, sending, and reporting on surveys is covered in [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys).
- **Task List.** A checklist you work through. It is not an agent. An agent is a workflow that can run on its own. A task list is a list of items to complete.
- **File Upload** and **External Link.** Files people attach, and links out to Drive or the web.

## How they get created

Most artifacts start in one of three ways:

- **Ask Cora in chat.** For example, ask for a prep doc, an account brief, a dashboard, a presentation or QBR deck, or a page. Cora saves the result as an artifact.
- **An agent writes one.** When a task's output is a document, dashboard, presentation, or similar deliverable, that output is saved as an artifact on the account or workspace.
- **You build a survey template.** On the **Artifacts** page, click **New Template**, select **Survey**, and save it. See [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys) for the rest of that flow.

Generating an artifact counts as an [Action](/getting-started/actions-and-usage). Opening or downloading one Cora already created does not.

New documents, dashboards, and pages are private to you by default — see [Sharing](#sharing) below to change that.

## How to find them

On the **Artifacts** page you can:

- Filter by type with **Filters** (the **Artifact Type** list above)
- Search with **Search artifacts...**
- Open an item to read it
- Download a file when Cora offers **Download**
- Pin a dashboard to **Analytics** with **Pin to Analytics**

On an account, the **Artifacts** tab shows that account's items with the same types.

## Sharing

Documents, dashboards, and pages are **private to you by default**: only you can see one until you decide otherwise. There are two independent things you can do to widen access, and you can combine them:

- **Change visibility** between **Private** (you, plus anyone you've added below) and **Organization** (your whole team).
- **Add specific people**, regardless of which visibility you're on. Adding or removing someone never changes the Private/Organization setting — it just widens or narrows who, specifically, can see a private item.

### From chat

If you want something visible to your team right away, say so when you ask for it:

_"Save this as a doc on the account, and make it visible to the whole team."_

You can also change visibility, or add and remove specific people, later without leaving chat, as long as you're the one who created it:

_"Share my Q3 QBR summary with Alex."_
_"Make that dashboard visible to the whole team."_
_"Take Alex off that doc."_

### From the Artifacts page

1. Open **Artifacts**, find the item, and select the **Share** icon on its row — or open the item's preview and select **Share** there.
2. Under **General access**, choose:
   - **Private** — only you, plus anyone you've added below. This is the default.
   - **Everyone in org** — visible to your whole team.
3. Under **People**, add teammates by name or email to give them access directly. This works the same way whether you're set to **Private** or **Everyone in org** — it doesn't switch you out of either one.

### Share with someone at a customer account

You can give a person at a customer account access to a document or page you saved on that account. They see it on their **Artifacts** page in the customer portal and can read it. They can't edit it.

1. Save the document or page on the account, or open it from that account's **Artifacts** tab.
2. Select **Share**, then under **People** pick the person. People at the account are labeled **Customer**.
3. Cora warns that this person is outside your company. Select **Share** to confirm.

Or ask Cora: _"Share the onboarding plan with `<email-address>`."_ Give Cora the email address. The email should be listed on the account team with Customer access.

## Templates

Mark a document, presentation, or page as a **template** when you want it reused, such as a standard QBR deck or a renewal brief. Agents running on your accounts can read your templates and copy them into a new document, deck, or page for that account. They never change the template itself.

Everything else you save stays private to your team. An account's agents see only what you mark as a template, whatever its sharing setting.

### From the Artifacts page

1. Open **Artifacts**, find the item, and open its preview.
2. Select **Use as template**. The item shows a **Template** label.

Select **Use as template** again to turn it off.

### From chat

Ask Cora:

_"Make my QBR deck a template."_
_"Stop treating my QBR deck as a template."_

You can also mark one as you create it: _"Create a QBR agenda and save it as a template."_

You can only mark a template on your own organization, not on a customer account, and only if you created the item or you're an Admin. Dashboards can't be templates. Surveys and checklists have their own templates; see [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys). To build a deck from a template, see [Slide Decks](/using-cora/slide-decks#reuse-a-deck-as-a-template).

## Who can edit

Sharing a document lets someone read it. It does not let them change it.

- A document, dashboard, presentation, or page a person created can be edited or deleted by that person or an Admin. Pinning a dashboard to Analytics is an edit.
- Something Cora wrote, including an account brief, can be edited by anyone who can already open it. Only an Admin can delete it.
- A template is read only for agents running on your accounts. They copy it; they cannot change or delete it.
- If you can open an item but cannot change it, the page is view only and names who can.
- A customer you share with can read the item and never edit it. Handing an item to a new owner works for teammates only.

Dashboards, presentations, and pages are edited in chat. Documents can also be edited on the page.

Your change applies right away.

<Note>
  You'll only see **Share** on artifacts you created. Cora can only change visibility or people-access on artifacts you own — someone else's private document or dashboard won't show up for you until they add you.
</Note>

## Data apps

A dashboard is a small data app Cora builds for you. Use it to work, not just to read: a review queue, a renewal tracker, a triage board.

It shows on **Artifacts** under the **Dashboard** filter, and you can pin it to **Analytics**.

Ask Cora in chat for one. Describe the app you want, for example: _"Build a renewal tracker for accounts renewing this quarter, where I can assign an owner and create a follow-up task."_

### What a data app can do

- Show charts, tables, and metrics over your accounts, tasks, signals, and connected tools.
- Let you filter and drill in, when you ask Cora for it.
- Sort and export its tables. Tables come with CSV export unless you ask Cora to leave it out.
- Act on a row in Cora: approve or reject a task, mark it complete, assign it, or create a task or checklist.
- Stay current. Tasks and agent runs sync within about 15 minutes. Connected tools, such as Slack or Salesforce, sync within about an hour. Short lists also read live data when the app opens, so a change made elsewhere shows up right away.

### Taking action

Every action shows what it will change and waits for you to confirm. Opening an app never changes anything.

An action runs as you, with your access, in your workspace. It cannot reach another workspace's data.

One action runs at a time. A second click while one is running answers "Another action is already running."

The change shows in the app the moment it finishes.

### Who can act

Admins and Operators can act in any data app they can open, with the same permissions they have in chat.

A Viewer can open a shared data app and see its data. An action that changes something answers "View-only access can't change data." A lookup still works, and it shows only customer-facing tasks.

Customers do not have data apps in the customer portal.

Change a data app in chat, as described in [Who can edit](#who-can-edit).

## Pages

Ask Cora in chat for an HTML page, such as a renewal page. Cora saves it on **Artifacts**.

It opens inside Cora. It is not a public website. Share it the same way as a document, including with someone at the customer account.

Images you upload in chat are saved with the page. A link to another site opens in a new tab. A link to a section of the page stays on the page.

**Export PDF** downloads the page as it looks when it opens. Anything that appears only after a click is not in the file. Asking Cora to email the page sends that PDF.

A page can include its own layout and behavior. It does not load scripts, frames, or forms from other sites.

Mark it as a template the same way as a document or presentation.

## Slide decks vs artifacts

Native presentations live on the **Artifacts** page, same as documents and dashboards. Chat shows a preview. Open the artifact to present.

[Google Slides](/using-cora/slide-decks) is still the path when you bring a Drive template or ask for a Google Slides copy. Those copies live in Drive and may not appear on **Artifacts**.
