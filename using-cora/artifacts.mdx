Artifacts are the saved outputs Cora keeps for you: briefs, dashboards, presentations, surveys, checklists, files, and links. Agents and chat create most of them. You open them later from the **Artifacts** page.

For how artifacts sit next to signals and agents, see [Core Concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts).

## What an artifact is

An artifact is a saved output attached to an account or to the workspace.

Open **Artifacts** from the nav to see everything in the workspace. Open an account and go to its **Artifacts** tab to see only that account's items.

Artifacts are not agent runs, and they are not signals. A run is one execution of a workflow. A signal is an event Cora detected. An artifact is the thing you keep.

## Types

Use **Filters** on the **Artifacts** page to show specific types. The filter labels are:

- **Document.** Markdown you can read in Cora, such as a meeting prep doc, a QBR summary, or an account brief. Chat and agents both write these.
- **Dashboard.** Live charts and tables over your warehouse data. Ask Cora in chat when you want one. You can pin a dashboard to **Analytics**.
- **Presentation.** An in-app slide deck, such as a QBR or a book review. Ask Cora in chat when you want one. Chat shows a preview. Reopen it from **Artifacts** with the **Presentation** filter, then **Present** from the player. You cannot pin a presentation to **Analytics**; that stays dashboards.
- **Survey** and **Survey Response.** A **Survey** is the reusable template. A **Survey Response** is a completed set of answers, attached to the account you sent the link for. Building, sending, and reporting on surveys is covered in [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys).
- **Task List.** A checklist you work through. It is not an agent. An agent is a workflow that can run on its own. A task list is a list of items to complete.
- **File Upload** and **External Link.** Files people attach, and links out to Drive or the web.

## How they get created

Most artifacts start in one of three ways:

- **Ask Cora in chat.** For example, ask for a prep doc, an account brief, a dashboard, or a presentation or QBR deck. Cora saves the result as an artifact.
- **An agent writes one.** When a task's output is a document, dashboard, presentation, or similar deliverable, that output is saved as an artifact on the account or workspace.
- **You build a survey template.** On the **Artifacts** page, click **New Template**, select **Survey**, and save it. See [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys) for the rest of that flow.

Generating an artifact counts as an [Action](/getting-started/actions-and-usage). Opening or downloading one Cora already created does not.

## How to find them

On the **Artifacts** page you can:

- Filter by type with **Filters** (the **Artifact Type** list above)
- Search with **Search artifacts...**
- Open an item to read it
- Download a file when Cora offers **Download**
- Pin a dashboard to **Analytics** with **Pin to Analytics**

On an account, the **Artifacts** tab shows that account's items with the same types.

## Sharing

Artifacts in your workspace are visible to your team. Anyone who can open **Artifacts** or the account can open the items there.

## Slide decks vs artifacts

Native presentations live on the **Artifacts** page, same as documents and dashboards. Chat shows a preview. Open the artifact to present.

[Google Slides](/using-cora/slide-decks) is still the path when you bring a Drive template or ask for a Google Slides copy. Those copies live in Drive and may not appear on **Artifacts**.
