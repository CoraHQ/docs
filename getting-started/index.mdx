This section covers the core setup steps to get your Cora workspace ready for your team. You will learn how to import accounts, invite users, connect your knowledge sources, and understand how Cora measures usage.

If you are new to the product nouns (signals, agents, artifacts), start with [Core Concepts](/getting-started/core-concepts). If you are setting up Cora for the first time, start with [Managing Your Accounts](/getting-started/managing-accounts), then add your team and knowledge base.
