<Update label="September 22, 2026" description="Private artifacts" tags={["New"]}>

Documents and dashboards are **private to you by default**. Only you can see one until you share it from chat or from the **Artifacts** page.

On **Artifacts**, open **Share** and choose under **General access**:

- **Only me** — private (the default)
- **Specific people** — teammates you pick by name or email
- **Everyone in org** — visible to your whole team

Learn more in [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).

</Update>

<Update label="September 20, 2026" description="Native presentations" tags={["New"]}>

Ask Cora in chat for a presentation or QBR deck. Cora saves it as a Presentation artifact.

Chat shows a preview. Reopen it from **Artifacts** with the **Presentation** filter, then **Present** from the player. You do not need a Google Drive connection.

Google Slides templates still work when you want a Drive copy. See [Slide decks](/using-cora/slide-decks).

</Update>

<Update label="September 16, 2026" description="Reconnect Gmail or Calendar" tags={["Improved"]}>

When Gmail or Google Calendar keeps failing, Cora pauses that connection and shows a notice on the homepage so you can reconnect instead of finding out from a stalled sync.

</Update>

<Update label="September 14, 2026" description="Communications feed" tags={["New"]}>

On an account's Signals tab, a Communications view shows a chronological feed of emails, meetings, calls, notes, and Slack, with a way to open the item.

</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" description="Nested tasks" tags={["New"]}>

You can add sub-tasks under a task, the way a checklist breaks work into smaller items.

</Update>
