<Update label="SHIP_DATE" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Build data apps with Cora

  Dashboards are now apps you work from, not just charts you read. Ask Cora for a review queue, a renewal tracker, or a triage board, with buttons on each row that approve, assign, complete, or create tasks. Every button shows what it will change before it runs, and the app updates the moment you confirm. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts#data-apps).
</Update>

<Update label="November 8, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Agent categories

  Group agents on the **Agents** page by what they are for. Admins add one with **New category**, and you can choose **Cora suggests a category for each** under **Uncategorized** so Cora sets them after you confirm. [Managing agents](/agents/managing-and-refining-agents#categories).
</Update>

<Update label="November 6, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Viewer role

  Viewer is a read-only role for people who follow accounts without changing them, like AEs. They can open accounts, signals, analytics, and shared artifacts, but can't edit, chat, run tasks, or build agents. [Team](/getting-started/managing-team).
</Update>

<Update label="October 29, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Pages

  Ask Cora for an HTML page, such as a renewal page. Reopen it from **Artifacts**, filter to **Page**, share it like a document, and use **Export PDF** to download the page as it looks when it opens. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts#pages).
</Update>

<Update label="October 22, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Share artifacts with customers

  Share a document on a customer account with someone. Select **Share** and add them under **People**, or ask in chat; they read it on **Artifacts** in the customer portal, can't edit it, and **Everyone in org** never includes customers. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts#share-with-someone-at-a-customer-account).
</Update>

<Update label="October 17, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Account briefs

  Set one up from **Accounts**, choose **All accounts** or **Matching rules**, and see what each source **Feeds**. Briefs refresh daily, and each brief written uses 1 Action. [Account briefs](/using-cora/account-briefs).
</Update>

<Update label="October 17, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Column scope and permissions

  AI columns get the same **Which accounts** choice, **All accounts** or **Matching rules**, from **Configure table columns** on **Accounts**. Adding, editing, reordering, recomputing, and deleting columns is Admin-only. [Custom columns](/using-cora/custom-columns).
</Update>

<Update label="October 15, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Artifact templates

  Mark a document or presentation as a template so agents on your accounts can copy it into new ones. Select **Use as template** in the preview, or ask in chat, and accounts never change the template. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts#templates).
</Update>

<Update label="October 11, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Who can change a saved artifact or agent

  You or Admins edit what you made, and sharing only lets others read. What Cora wrote is editable by anyone who can open it, and only an Admin deletes it or changes an agent Cora set up. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts#who-can-edit), [Managing agents](/agents/managing-and-refining-agents#editing-agent-configuration).
</Update>

<Update label="October 11, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Slack agent

  Once Cora is in a thread, it follows the conversation. It replies when a message is for it, stays out of side conversations, and reacts when a short acknowledgment is enough.
</Update>

<Update label="October 9, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Live monitoring

  When an agent's checks fail on a finished run, you get one email an hour. Choose **Investigate with Cora** in the email, or ask in chat why the checks failed. [Performance](/agents/agent-performance).
</Update>

<Update label="October 8, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Column explanations

  Hover an AI score and see why Cora gave it. New AI columns include the reason from the start, and for one you already have, ask Cora to turn explanations on. [Custom columns](/using-cora/custom-columns).
</Update>

<Update label="October 7, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Ask Cora

  Ask from the page you are already on. Click **Chat** in the header. Cora knows what is on screen, and the page updates after it makes a change.
</Update>

<Update label="September 22, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Private artifacts

  Documents and dashboards start private, so a draft stays with you until you share it. Share from chat or from **Artifacts**: **Only me**, **Specific people**, or **Everyone in org**. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).
</Update>

<Update label="September 21, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Inline previews

  When Cora writes a doc or deck in chat, read it in the thread and reply there. You do not have to open another tab. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).
</Update>

<Update label="September 20, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Native presentations

  Ask for a QBR deck in chat and present it from Cora, with no Google Slides template or Drive connection. Reopen from **Artifacts**, filter to **Presentation**, click **Present**, and use Google Slides when you want a Drive copy. [Slide decks](/using-cora/slide-decks).
</Update>

<Update label="September 18, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Create from an account

  On an account, create the task or doc for that account without leaving the page. You do not have to open Tasks or Artifacts and attach the account again.
</Update>

<Update label="September 14, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Communications feed

  Before a call, see recent emails, meetings, calls, notes, and Slack in one place. On the account, open **Signals**, switch to **Communications**, and open any item.
</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Nested tasks

  Break a task into the steps someone actually does. Open the task and add sub-tasks under it, so "prep the QBR" can hold "pull the usage" and "draft the agenda".
</Update>

<Update label="August 31, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Web research

  Cora checks public sources each week and files what it finds on the account. Expansion news shows up as a signal, so you do not go looking for it.
</Update>

<Update label="August 24, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Home

  Open Cora and see what needs you today. Start there, instead of starting from a list of accounts, so the work that needs you is in front of you when you arrive.
</Update>

<Update label="August 23, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Call recordings

  Connect Attention or Granola from **Settings**, then **Organization Settings**, then **Integrations**. Recorded calls and notes show up as signals, matched to accounts by attendee email. [Attention](/integrations/attention), [Granola](/integrations/granola).
</Update>

<Update label="July 24, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Drive links in chat

  Paste a Google Drive link in chat and click **Grant** on the card. Cora reads that file and keeps access to it, and you do not grant your whole Drive. [Google Drive](/integrations/gmail-google-drive#use-drive-files-in-chat-and-agents).
</Update>

<Update label="July 23, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Surveys

  Build an NPS or CSAT survey on **Artifacts** with **New Template**, then **Generate Link** for one person. Each response comes back on that account as a **Survey Response**. [Surveys](/using-cora/surveys).
</Update>

<Update label="May 27, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Custom columns

  Add health and other columns on **Accounts** with **Configure table columns**, then **Add custom column**. Bands such as Healthy or At risk sit on the grid, not only an AI score. [Custom columns](/using-cora/custom-columns).
</Update>

<Update label="May 8, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Intercom

  Connect Intercom from **Settings**, then **Organization Settings**, then **Integrations**, and support conversations can become signals. Turn **Lifecycle Events** on so Cora can read those threads. [Intercom](/integrations/intercom).
</Update>

<Update label="May 5, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Snowflake

  Connect Snowflake from **Settings**, then **Organization Settings**, then **Integrations**, and use that warehouse data in Cora. Cora queries your views on a schedule for agents, dashboards, and workflows. [Snowflake](/integrations/snowflake).
</Update>

<Update label="May 3, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Dashboards

  Ask for a dashboard in chat and Cora saves the live charts and tables as an artifact. Pin it to **Analytics** with **Pin to Analytics** on **Artifacts**. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).
</Update>

<Update label="April 27, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Documents

  Ask for a brief or prep doc in chat and Cora saves it as a document you can open again. Find it on **Artifacts** and filter to **Document**. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).
</Update>

<Update label="April 13, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## HubSpot

  Connect HubSpot from **Settings**, then **Organization Settings**, then **Integrations**, and bring companies in as accounts. You can import them from **Accounts** after the card shows **Connected**. [HubSpot](/integrations/hubspot).
</Update>

<Update label="April 2, 2026" tags={["New releases"]}>
  ## Skills

  Write a skill in chat and agents follow those instructions when you ask. Save it from the conversation, then run it by name or from **Settings** under **Skills**. [How to build skills](/agents/how-to-build-skills).
</Update>

<Update label="March 30, 2026" tags={["Improvements"]}>
  ## Gmail aliases

  Send from a dedicated Cora address instead of only your own. It has to be a full Google Workspace user, not a group or alias, and you share that address with your implementation lead to connect it. [Email alias](/getting-started/email-alias).
</Update>
