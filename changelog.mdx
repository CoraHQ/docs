<Update label="September 22, 2026" description="Private artifacts" tags={["New"]}>

Documents and dashboards start private, so a draft brief or QBR stays with you until you want the team to see it. Share it from chat or from **Artifacts** when it is ready: **Only me**, **Specific people**, or **Everyone in org**. See [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).

</Update>

<Update label="September 21, 2026" description="Inline previews" tags={["Improved"]}>

When Cora writes a doc or deck in chat, read it in the thread and keep going. You do not have to open another tab to see what it made.

</Update>

<Update label="September 20, 2026" description="Native presentations" tags={["New"]}>

Ask for a QBR deck in chat and present it from Cora, without a Google Slides template or a Drive connection. Chat shows a preview. Reopen it from **Artifacts** with the **Presentation** filter, then **Present**. Google Slides still works when you want a copy in Drive. See [Slide decks](/using-cora/slide-decks).

</Update>

<Update label="September 18, 2026" description="Create from an account" tags={["New"]}>

On an account, start the task or doc for that account right there. You do not have to leave the account and attach it again from Tasks or Artifacts.

</Update>

<Update label="September 14, 2026" description="Communications feed" tags={["New"]}>

Before a call, open the account and see recent emails, meetings, calls, notes, and Slack in order, instead of checking each tool. Switch to **Communications** on the account's **Signals** tab and open any item.

</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" description="Nested tasks" tags={["New"]}>

Break a task into the steps someone actually does, so "prep the QBR" can hold "pull the usage" and "draft the agenda" instead of one vague to-do.

</Update>
