<Update label="September 22, 2026" description="Private artifacts" tags={["New"]}>

Documents and dashboards start private, so a draft brief or QBR stays with you until you want the team to see it. When it is ready, share it from chat or from **Artifacts**: **Only me**, **Specific people**, or **Everyone in org**. [Artifacts](/using-cora/artifacts).

</Update>

<Update label="September 21, 2026" description="Inline previews" tags={["Improved"]}>

When Cora writes a doc or deck in chat, read it in the thread and reply there. You do not have to open another tab.

</Update>

<Update label="September 20, 2026" description="Native presentations" tags={["New"]}>

Ask for a QBR deck in chat and present it from Cora, without a Google Slides template or a Drive connection. Reopen it from **Artifacts**, filter to **Presentation**, and click **Present**. Google Slides still works when you want a copy in Drive. [Slide decks](/using-cora/slide-decks).

</Update>

<Update label="September 18, 2026" description="Create from an account" tags={["New"]}>

On an account, create the task or doc for that account without leaving the page. You do not have to open Tasks or Artifacts and attach the account again.

</Update>

<Update label="September 14, 2026" description="Communications feed" tags={["New"]}>

Before a call, see recent emails, meetings, calls, notes, and Slack in one place. On the account, open **Signals**, switch to **Communications**, and open any item.

</Update>

<Update label="September 10, 2026" description="Nested tasks" tags={["New"]}>

Break a task into the steps someone actually does. Open the task and add sub-tasks under it, so "prep the QBR" can hold "pull the usage" and "draft the agenda".

</Update>
